Privacy notice

Last updated 21 October 2026. Private beta.

Ako is a learning app. This notice says what we store about you, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Who we are

Ako is run by [Antipodean Ventures Limited, company number XXXXXXXX], registered in England and Wales at [registered address]. For anything about your data, email [privacy@ako.example].

What we store

WhatWhy
Your name, email address and a hashed passwordTo run your account and let you sign in
The subjects you add, your plan for each, and your progress: topics learned, answers, scores, review cards and when you studiedTo teach you, schedule reviews and show you where you stand
What you tell us you already know (jobs, qualifications, languages, sports, hobbies, fields, tools), item by item, only after you confirm each oneSo lessons can build on what you know and skip what you can do
Transcripts of your written exchanges with the tutor and the role-play partnerTo mark them and to improve your next lesson
A count of how many lessons, marks and tutor turns you useTo run the service fairly and keep costs sane

We do not store payment details (there are none during the beta), your location, your contacts, or anything from your device beyond what you type into the app. If you later send a photo for a form check, it is sent to the model and not kept.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Ireland, eu-west-1). Access is restricted so that only you, signed in, can read your own rows.

The AI model

Lessons, marking, plans and the tutor are produced by Claude, a model made by Anthropic. To do that we send Anthropic the content needed for the task: the subject, the topic, your answers, your confirmed background summary and the lesson so far. Anthropic processes it in the United States under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on our data. We send no email addresses or names with those requests.

Email

We email you to confirm your address and to reset a password. During the beta we may also email you to ask what you think. No marketing lists, no third-party newsletters.

Cookies and analytics

The web app keeps a sign-in token in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics in the beta.

How long

Until you delete your account. Deleting it removes everything above at once; backups roll off within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. In the app, You, then Delete my data, does the last of those immediately. For the others, email the address above and we will answer within a month, usually much sooner. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we have got something wrong.

Age

Ako is for adults. Do not use it if you are under 18.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
